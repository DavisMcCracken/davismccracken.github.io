# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Hiring managers and recruiters** screening Davis for data analyst roles or other tech roles near his interests (data, automation, scripting, analytics). They skim fast, often from a link on a résumé or LinkedIn, and decide within a minute whether to read further.
- **Potential freelance or consulting clients**, including possible web development/design work. They want evidence he can deliver and a clear way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Davis McCracken that shows his work and gets him interviews or freelance inquiries. Success means a visitor understands what he does, trusts that the work is real, and contacts him or keeps his name in mind.

## Positioning

He is the Database Coordinator at Ronald McDonald House Louisville and works where the donor database, spreadsheets and scripts meet: rebuilding manual processes into dependable pipelines, reporting and statistical models. He is practical and moves from Excel to Python when the problem calls for it, rather than being a pure software engineer or a pure analyst.

## Operating Context

- Usually reached from a résumé, LinkedIn or GitHub link; read on desktop and on phones.
- Static site on GitHub Pages (`davismccracken.github.io`), built with Astro.
- Contact goes through Web3Forms when a key is configured, otherwise email (davisnmccracken@gmail.com). Also on GitHub (DavisMcCracken) and LinkedIn (davis-mccracken).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero, work (project timeline), about, currently, links, contact. Each project has a case-study page at `/work/<id>/`.
- Projects are Markdown files in `src/content/projects/`.
- The projects are internal nonprofit work on donor data. Screenshots, data and metrics must be anonymised or approved before publishing.
- Undecided: final project list and the public facts for each (owner will supply them last); résumé PDF; Web3Forms key; Open Graph image.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: dry, lowercase, slightly terminal-flavoured humour, kept tight and never at the expense of clarity.
- Owner-chosen palette: Tokyo Night (replacing the earlier Catppuccin palette).
- Existing logo mark: `public/logo-mark.png`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three project drafts (sales process revamp, donations data pipeline, churn & high-value prediction). Their impact figures are unverified placeholders, marked `TODO(davis)`. Do not present them as fact or invent new ones.
- No testimonials, photos, client list or résumé yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Real work over decoration: every effect must earn its place, and evidence beats claims.
2. Scannable in under a minute for a recruiter, with depth one click away in the case studies.
3. Personality through voice and detail, not gimmicks.
4. Honest by default: no invented metrics, clients or testimonials.
